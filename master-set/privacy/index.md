# Master Set Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 5, 2026*

## Summary

Master Set does not collect personal data. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking. Your collection, lists and settings stay on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage, which we cannot read.

This policy covers the Master Set app for iPhone and iPad, made by Steven Lu ("we").

## Your collection stays with you

The cards you own, your lists and your settings are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, the app also keeps a copy in your own private iCloud database so it can sync between your devices. That database belongs to your Apple Account: only you can see it, and we have no access to it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

Card images the app downloads are saved on your device so binders open quickly and work offline.

## What the app sends to load cards

To show sets, cards and prices, the app asks our server for them. These requests never include your name, email, an account, an advertising or device ID, or anything about your collection.

- **Catalog requests:** which set, Pokédex entry or search you opened. When you search, the words you type are sent to our server, which looks them up with our search provider, [Algolia](https://www.algolia.com/policies/privacy/). Algolia receives only the search words, not your IP address.
- **Card images:** downloaded from our image storage on Cloudflare.

Like any website, our server and hosting providers ([Railway](https://railway.com/legal/privacy) and [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)) receive your IP address and basic device details, such as the iOS version, with each request. Our server logs record the time and address requested, including search words, but not your IP address, so a logged search can't be tied to you. We use these logs only to keep the service running and secure, and they are deleted automatically after a short period.

## What we don't do

- No accounts or sign-in
- No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking
- No advertising, and no tracking across other apps or websites
- No selling, renting or sharing of personal data, because we don't collect any

## Links to other sites

The TCGplayer button on a card opens that card on TCGplayer, in Safari or the TCGplayer app. The app adds nothing about you to that link. Once you're there, [TCGplayer's privacy policy](https://www.tcgplayer.com/privacy-policy) applies.

## Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device. To remove the copy in iCloud, go to Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage, choose Master Set and delete its data. We hold nothing else about you to delete.

## Children

Master Set is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13.
